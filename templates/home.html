Eliminating Uncertainty in Agricultural Marketing

For Farmers

  • Guaranteed Off-Take: Secure buyers weeks before harvest to avoid distressing spot market sell-offs.
  • Advance Liquidity: Contract activation triggers an advance deposit to help manage harvesting costs.
  • Transparent Fair Pricing: Transparent bilateral counter-offers eliminate predatory commission agents.

For Agricultural Buyers

  • Assured Quality Standards: Enforce strict moisture, purity, and grading specifications in the contract.
  • Procurement Certainty: Lock supply volumes and predictable pricing directly from producer farms.
  • Escrow-Protected Fulfillment: Milestone payouts released only upon weighbridge scale confirmation.
